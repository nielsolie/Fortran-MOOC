Welcome to Week 5 of Fortran for Scientific Computing.

During last week you learned what influences the performance of your code, and
how to best exploit modern hardware.

In this last week, we will introduce you to some libraries that are pervasive
in scientific computing. Being familiar with those will save you a lot of time
and ensure that your application are built on a solid computational foundation.

We will discuss libraries for linear algebra, as well as HDF5, a library/file
format to store scientific data.

Finally, you will get an overview of the options for parallel programming in
Fortran. This is but a brief introduction, and we encourage you to register for
additional courses on this topic offered by PRACE.
